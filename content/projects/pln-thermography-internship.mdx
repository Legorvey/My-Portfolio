## Context

This was an internship at PT PLN (Persero) ULTG Bandung Barat in July and August 2026, as a Power Transmission Engineer Intern. The main task was thermographic inspection of 150 kV transmission towers.

## Inspection method

I inspected jumper clamps and repair sleeves for thermal anomalies. For each reading I calculated the temperature differential against a 32C ambient baseline. The differential decided how severe the anomaly was and how urgently maintenance should be scheduled.

## Findings

- **Tower 22:** a critical overheating fault with a 35.18C differential. Immediate corrective action was recommended to prevent conductor failure.
- **Tower 37:** a secondary warning with an 18.00C differential.
- **Root causes:** the root cause analysis pointed to loosening from mechanical vibration and failures in hydraulic crimping as the main factors.

## Field maintenance

I also assisted with corrective maintenance in the field:

- Grounding work on tower footing resistance
- Replacement of high-voltage insulators
- Cleaning of hotspots
